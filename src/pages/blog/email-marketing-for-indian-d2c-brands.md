## Why email still matters for Indian D2C brands

Email remains the highest‑ROI channel for direct‑to‑consumer businesses in India. Unlike social platforms that throttle reach, an owned list lets you speak to customers on your terms. For email marketing for Indian D2C brands, the key is treating the inbox as a relationship channel, not a broadcast pipe.

## Build a clean list from day one

- Use double opt‑in on every signup form.
- Offer a clear value exchange: early access, useful guides, or a modest discount.
- Remove hard bounces and inactive subscribers quarterly.

A healthy list protects deliverability and keeps costs low on platforms that charge per contact.

## Segment before you send

Blanket blasts dilute relevance. Segment by purchase history, browsing behavior, geographic tier (metro vs. tier‑2), and engagement level. A Hyderabad‑based skincare brand, for example, can send monsoon‑care tips to customers in coastal cities while promoting sunscreen to those in drier regions.

## Automate the right journeys

Set up three core flows:

1. **Welcome series** – introduce brand story, best‑sellers, and a first‑order incentive.
2. **Abandoned cart** – timely reminder with product image and a limited‑time offer.
3. **Post‑purchase** – order confirmation, usage tips, and a request for review after delivery.

Each flow should have 2‑3 emails spaced 1‑3 days apart, with clear CTAs and an easy unsubscribe link.

## Write for the inbox, not the boardroom

Subject lines under 50 characters, pre‑header text that complements the subject, and a single primary CTA per email increase click‑through rates. Use conversational Hindi‑English blends where it feels natural; authenticity beats polished corporate speak.

## Measure what moves revenue

Track these metrics weekly:

- Open rate (benchmark 20‑25% for D2C)
- Click‑through rate (target 2‑3%)
- Revenue per email (RPE)
- Unsubscribe rate (keep <0.5%)

Tie each campaign to a UTM‑tagged landing page so you can attribute sales directly.

## Keep deliverability high

Authenticate with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. Warm new IPs gradually. Monitor spam complaints via feedback loops and suppress complainers instantly. A clean sending reputation ensures your emails land in the primary tab, not promotions or spam.
