## Why Meta ads matter for Hyderabad businesses
Hyderabad’s market blends traditional commerce with a fast‑growing digital audience. Meta (Facebook & Instagram) ads give local brands a direct line to people who already spend hours on these platforms. When the targeting is set to the city, its neighborhoods, and relevant interests, the spend goes toward prospects who can actually walk into a store or place an online order.

## Setting up a solid foundation
- Define a clear campaign objective: sales, leads, or brand awareness.
- Install the Meta pixel on your website and configure conversion events.
- Build a custom audience from past customers, website visitors, and email subscribers.

## Targeting that works locally
Start with a geographic radius of 10‑15 km around your business address. Layer interest categories such as “food & dining”, “fashion”, or “tech gadgets” depending on your niche. Add a look‑alike audience based on your best customers to expand reach without losing relevance.

## Creative that converts
- Use authentic photos of your product, team, or storefront; stock images feel distant.
- Keep video under 15 seconds, front‑load the hook, and add captions for sound‑off viewing.
- Include a single, unmistakable call‑to‑action: “Shop now”, “Book a table”, or “Get a quote”.

## Budgeting and bidding basics
Set a daily budget that lets the algorithm gather at least 50 conversions per week before you evaluate performance. Choose “Lowest cost” bidding for most direct‑response goals; switch to “Cost cap” only when you have a firm CPA target.

## Measuring what matters
Track cost per result, return on ad spend, and frequency. If frequency climbs above 3, refresh creative. Pause under‑performing ad sets and reallocate budget to the top 20 % that deliver the best ROAS.

## Quick checklist before you launch
- Pixel firing on all key events
- Audience size between 100 k and 1 M
- At least three creative variations per ad set
- UTM parameters on every destination URL
- Test the checkout or lead form on mobile
