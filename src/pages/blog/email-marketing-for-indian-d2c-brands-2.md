## Why Email Still Matters for Indian D2C Brands
Email remains the highest‑ROI channel for direct‑to‑consumer businesses in India. Unlike social algorithms, you own the list and control the message. For founders in Hyderabad and across the country, a disciplined email program turns one‑time buyers into repeat customers without relying on paid ads.

## Build a Clean List First
Start with permission‑based sign‑ups: website forms, checkout opt‑ins, and offline events. Use double opt‑in to keep bounce rates low and protect sender reputation. Regularly scrub inactive addresses — quarterly is a good cadence — so deliverability stays strong.

## Segment Like a Pro
Break the list into meaningful groups: first‑time buyers, high‑value repeaters, cart abandoners, and lapsed customers. Tailor content for each segment — product recommendations for repeaters, win‑back offers for lapsed users, and educational series for new subscribers. Segmentation drives higher open and click rates than a single blast.

## Automate the Right Journeys
Set up core automations once and let them run:
- Welcome series (3‑5 emails) that introduces brand story and best sellers.
- Abandoned cart reminder (2 emails, 1 hour and 24 hours later).
- Post‑purchase follow‑up (review request, care tips, cross‑sell).
- Re‑engagement flow for subscribers inactive 60‑90 days.
Automation saves time and ensures timely, relevant touches.

## Design for Mobile and Deliverability
Most Indian consumers read mail on phones. Use single‑column layouts, large tap targets, and concise copy. Keep subject lines under 50 characters, avoid spammy words, and authenticate with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. Test each send with a seed list before going live.

## Measure What Moves the Needle
Track metrics that tie to revenue: open rate, click‑through rate, conversion rate, and revenue per email. Set benchmarks based on your own history, not industry averages. Use UTM parameters to attribute sales in Google Analytics or your e‑commerce dashboard.

## Quick Checklist for Your Next Campaign
- Verify list hygiene and consent records.
- Define segment and goal for the send.
- Write a clear, benefit‑driven subject line.
- Build mobile‑first creative with a single CTA.
- Schedule send time based on audience activity data.
- Run a deliverability test and preview on major clients.
- Analyse results within 48 hours and note learnings.
