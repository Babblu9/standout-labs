## Claim and Verify Your Google Business Profile
Start by claiming your Business Profile on Google. If you haven’t done this, search for your business name on Google Maps, click ‘Claim this business,’ and follow the verification steps — usually a postcard sent to your Hyderabad address or a phone call. A verified profile unlocks every other optimisation.

## Optimize Core Information
Fill every field: business name, category, address, phone, website, and hours. Use the exact name you operate under; avoid keyword stuffing. Choose the most specific primary category (e.g., ‘Italian restaurant’ not just ‘Restaurant’). Add secondary categories that match services you actually offer. Keep NAP (name, address, phone) identical across the web.

## Build Consistent Local Citations
Submit your NAP to reputable Indian directories — Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, and local Hyderabad chambers of commerce. Consistency matters more than quantity; a mismatch in a single digit can confuse Google's algorithm. Use a spreadsheet to track each listing and audit quarterly.

## Encourage and Manage Reviews
Ask satisfied customers to leave a review on your Google profile. Respond to every review, positive or negative, within 24 hours. A steady flow of genuine reviews signals relevance and trust. Never buy reviews; Google detects patterns and penalises the profile.

## Leverage Local Content and Photos
Upload high-resolution photos of your storefront, interior, products, and team. Add geo-tagged images when possible. Publish short posts about Hyderabad events, seasonal offers, or community involvement. Fresh, location-specific content keeps the profile active and improves map-pack visibility.

## Monitor Performance and Adjust
Use the Insights tab in Google Business Profile to see how customers find you — direct search, discovery search, or map views. Track actions: calls, direction requests, website clicks. If discovery searches are low, revisit categories, add more photos, or earn additional citations. Iterate monthly.
