## Why local SEO matters for Hyderabad businesses
Local search drives foot traffic and appointments for brick‑and‑mortar shops and clinics. When someone searches “dentist near me” or “hardware store Hyderabad”, Google shows results based on proximity, relevance, and prominence. Optimising for those signals puts your business in front of high‑intent customers without paying for ads.

## Claim and optimise your Google Business Profile
Start by claiming the free Google Business Profile (GBP). Fill every field: exact business name, address, phone, category, hours, and a concise description that includes your primary service and location. Add high‑resolution photos of the storefront, interior, staff, and any certifications. Enable messaging and keep the Q&A section updated.

## Build consistent local citations
Citations are mentions of your NAP (name, address, phone) on directories, industry sites, and local blogs. Submit to India‑specific platforms like Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, and local Hyderabad chambers of commerce. Ensure the NAP matches exactly what appears on your GBP and website — down to abbreviations and punctuation.

## Encourage genuine reviews
Reviews influence both ranking and conversion. Ask satisfied patients or shoppers to leave a review on Google, Practo, or Zomato, depending on your vertical. Provide a simple link in follow‑up SMS or email. Respond to every review, thanking the reviewer and addressing concerns publicly; this signals activity to Google.

## Optimise on‑page signals for local intent
Add location‑specific schema markup (LocalBusiness) to your site. Include the city name in title tags, headings, and the first paragraph of service pages. Create separate pages for each neighbourhood you serve (e.g., “Banjara Hills dental clinic”). Embed a Google Map with your exact pin.

## Track what matters
Use Google Search Console and GBP Insights to monitor impressions, clicks, direction requests, and calls. Set up UTM‑tagged links for any offline campaigns so you can attribute walk‑ins. Review the data monthly and adjust categories, photos, or keyword focus based on what drives the most qualified leads.

## Quick checklist
- Claim and verify Google Business Profile
- Match NAP across all citations
- Add schema markup and local keywords on site
- Collect and reply to reviews weekly
- Monitor GBP Insights and Search Console monthly
