## Why email still matters for Indian D2C brands

Indian direct‑to‑consumer companies often chase the latest social trend, but email remains the highest‑ROI channel when done right. It gives you a direct line to customers who have already shown intent, and it works even when platform algorithms shift. For founders in Hyderabad and across the country, a disciplined email program can turn one‑time buyers into repeat advocates without relying on paid media.

## Build a clean list from day one

- Use double opt‑in on every signup form.
- Capture source (website, offline event, referral) to tailor later messaging.
- Remove hard bounces and inactive subscribers after 90 days of no opens.

A healthy list protects deliverability and keeps costs low on platforms that charge per contact.

## Segment like a local market

India’s diversity means a single broadcast rarely resonates. Start with three core segments:

1. **New subscribers** – welcome series that introduces brand story and first‑order incentive.
2. **Active buyers** – product recommendations based on purchase history and regional festivals.
3. **Lapsed customers** – re‑engagement flow with a time‑limited offer or a quick survey.

Layer on geographic tags (e.g., “Hyderabad metro”, “Tier‑2 South”) so you can reference local events, weather, or language nuances without sounding generic.

## Automate the right journeys

Set up workflows that trigger on behavior, not just calendar dates:

- **Abandoned cart** – send within 30 minutes, follow up at 24 hours with a small discount.
- **Post‑purchase** – order confirmation, shipping update, then a review request after delivery.
- **Birthday/anniversary** – a personalized coupon code valid for 7 days.

Keep each flow under five emails; longer sequences fatigue Indian inboxes that already receive heavy promotional volume.

## Keep deliverability high in India

Indian ISPs (Airtel, Jio, BSNL) filter aggressively. Follow these basics:

- Authenticate with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.
- Warm new sending IPs gradually — start with 5 % of your list.
- Monitor spam complaints; aim for <0.1 %.
- Use a recognizable “from” name (brand + “Team”) and a reply‑to address that is monitored.

Regularly run a seed‑list test to see where your mail lands before a major campaign.

## Measure what moves revenue

Open rates are vanity; focus on metrics that tie to cash:

- **Revenue per email (RPE)** – total sales attributed ÷ emails sent.
- **Conversion rate by segment** – shows which cohorts respond to which offers.
- **List growth rate** – net new subscribers minus unsubscribes and bounces.

Set a monthly review cadence. If a segment’s RPE drops for two consecutive months, pause the flow and test a new angle.

## Quick checklist for founders

- [ ] Double opt‑in enabled on all forms
- [ ] SPF/DKIM/DMARC configured
- [ ] Three core segments live
- [ ] Core automations (welcome, cart, post‑purchase) running
- [ ] Monthly RPE report scheduled

Consistent execution of these fundamentals puts your email program ahead of most competitors who treat it as an afterthought.
