## Claim and Verify Your Google Business Profile

Start by claiming your listing on Google Business Profile. If you haven’t verified yet, request a postcard or use phone/email verification. A verified profile unlocks editing rights, insights, and the ability to appear in the local map pack for Hyderabad searches.

## Optimize Core Information

Fill every field: business name, address, phone number, website, hours, and category. Use the exact name you operate under — no keyword stuffing. Choose the most specific primary category (e.g., “Italian restaurant” not just “restaurant”). Add secondary categories that reflect services. Upload high‑resolution photos of the storefront, interior, products, and team; profiles with photos get 42% more direction requests.

## Build Consistent Citations

Consistency across directories signals trust. Submit your NAP (name, address, phone) to major Indian platforms — Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, and local Hyderabad chambers of commerce. Keep the format identical: same abbreviations, same pin code. Audit quarterly with a citation tool to catch drift.

## Encourage and Manage Reviews

Reviews are the strongest social signal for map rankings. Ask satisfied customers at the point of sale — via QR code on the receipt or a follow‑up WhatsApp message. Respond to every review within 24 hours; thank positives, address negatives professionally. A steady flow of recent, keyword‑rich reviews pushes you higher in the Hyderabad map pack.

## Leverage Local Content and Signals

Publish location‑specific blog posts, case studies, or event pages that mention neighborhoods (Banjara Hills, Hitech City, Secunderabad). Embed a Google Map on your contact page. Add schema markup for LocalBusiness. Earn backlinks from Hyderabad news outlets, blogs, and partner sites. These signals tell Google you’re relevant to the city.
