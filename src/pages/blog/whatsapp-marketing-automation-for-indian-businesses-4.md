## Why WhatsApp matters for Indian businesses
WhatsApp is the default communication channel for millions of consumers across India. For a founder in Hyderabad or any other city, the platform offers direct access to a mobile‑first audience that checks messages dozens of times a day. Automation turns that reach into a scalable sales and support engine without hiring a large team.

## Choosing the right automation approach
There are three common paths: using the official WhatsApp Business API with a verified provider, leveraging a no‑code chatbot builder, or integrating a CRM that already supports WhatsApp templates. The API route gives full control and compliance but requires a technical setup. No‑code tools let you launch flows in hours, ideal for early‑stage ventures. CRM integration works well when you already manage leads in a system like HubSpot or Zoho.

## Core features to look for
- Template management for promotional and transactional messages
- Two‑way messaging with quick‑reply buttons and list messages
- Audience segmentation based on tags, purchase history, or location
- Scheduling and drip sequences that respect the 24‑hour customer care window
- Analytics dashboard showing delivery, read, and reply rates

## Setting up a compliant workflow
Start by registering a verified business account and submitting message templates for approval. Build opt‑in flows on your website, at checkout, or via QR codes in physical stores. Store consent records to stay within TRAI and Meta policies. Use the 24‑hour window for free‑form replies; after that, only approved templates can be sent. Automate fallback messages that guide users back into the window.

## Measuring what matters
Track delivery rate to catch number‑formatting issues early. Monitor read rate as a proxy for relevance. Reply rate and conversion events (link clicks, purchases, appointment bookings) reveal the true ROI. Set up UTM parameters on every outbound link so you can attribute revenue in Google Analytics or your attribution tool.

## Getting started today
Pick one use case — order confirmations, abandoned‑cart reminders, or appointment confirmations — and build a minimal viable flow. Test with a small segment, iterate on copy and timing, then expand. Document the process so future team members can replicate it across product lines or regions.
