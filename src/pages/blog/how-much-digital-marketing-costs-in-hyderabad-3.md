## What drives digital marketing costs in Hyderabad

Several factors shape the price you pay. The breadth of services — SEO, paid ads, content creation, social media management, email marketing, and website work — each adds a line item. Competition in your niche matters; highly contested keywords or audiences push up bid prices and content depth. The experience level of the team you hire also matters: a boutique agency with senior strategists charges more than a junior freelancer. Finally, the contract model (retainer, project‑based, performance‑based) changes the cash flow.

## Typical price bands for core services

- SEO retainer: ₹40,000‑₹1,50,000 per month depending on keyword difficulty and reporting depth.
- PPC management (Google Ads, Meta Ads): 10‑20 % of ad spend, with a minimum fee of ₹25,000‑₹50,000.
- Content marketing (blog posts, videos, infographics): ₹5,000‑₹20,000 per piece, or ₹30,000‑₹1,00,000 for a monthly content calendar.
- Social media management: ₹15,000‑₹80,000 per month for strategy, creation, scheduling, and community response.
- Email marketing setup and automation: ₹10,000‑₹40,000 one‑time plus ₹5,000‑₹15,000 monthly for list growth.
- Website development or redesign: ₹1,00,000‑₹5,00,000 for a custom site; maintenance adds ₹5,000‑₹20,000 monthly.

## How to set a realistic budget

Start with your revenue target and work backward. If you aim for ₹1 crore in new sales and expect a 5 % conversion from paid traffic, you need enough ad spend to generate the required clicks. Allocate 40‑50 % of the total marketing budget to paid channels, 20‑30 % to SEO and content, and the rest to creative, tools, and testing. Keep a 10‑15 % contingency for unexpected opportunities or algorithm changes.

## Choosing the right partner

Ask for a clear scope document, reporting cadence, and ownership of assets. Verify that the team has experience in your industry vertical, not just generic case studies. Prefer agencies that offer transparent pricing models and are willing to pilot a small project before a long‑term retainer. Communication style and timezone alignment are practical advantages when working with a Hyderabad‑based team.

## Quick checklist before you sign

- Defined KPIs and reporting format
- Itemised pricing with no hidden fees
- Ownership of content, accounts, and data
- Exit clause with notice period
- References from similar‑size businesses (ask for contacts, not just logos)
