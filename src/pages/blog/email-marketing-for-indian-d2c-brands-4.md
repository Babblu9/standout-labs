## Why email still matters for Indian D2C
Indian direct‑to‑consumer brands often chase social virality, but email remains the only channel you own outright. It delivers a predictable line to customers who have already shown intent, and it works even when platform algorithms shift. For founders in Hyderabad and across the country, a disciplined email program compounds faster than any paid boost.

## Build a clean list from day one
Collect addresses at every touchpoint — checkout, pop‑ups, offline events, WhatsApp opt‑ins — but always ask for explicit permission. Use double opt‑in to filter typos and bots. A smaller, engaged list outperforms a massive, cold one, especially when Indian ISPs scrutinize sender reputation.

## Segment before you send
One‑size‑fits‑all blasts waste budget and annoy subscribers. Segment by purchase history, browsing behavior, geographic tier (metro vs tier‑2), and engagement level. A first‑time buyer in Hyderabad gets a welcome series; a repeat buyer receives loyalty offers. Segmentation turns generic newsletters into relevant conversations.

## Automate the right journeys
Set up three core flows: welcome, abandoned cart, and post‑purchase. Keep each flow under five emails, spaced 1‑3 days apart. Use dynamic product blocks so the content reflects the exact SKU a shopper viewed. Automation frees the team to focus on strategy rather than manual sends.

## Design for mobile‑first inboxes
Over 80 % of Indian email opens happen on phones. Use single‑column layouts, large tap targets, and concise copy. Pre‑header text should extend the subject line, not repeat it. Test on Gmail, Outlook, and regional clients like Yahoo India before every launch.

## Measure what actually moves revenue
Open rates are vanity; track revenue per email, conversion rate, and list growth velocity. UTM parameters on every link let you attribute sales in Google Analytics or your attribution tool. Review weekly, but only act on trends that persist over a month.

## Keep deliverability high in the Indian ecosystem
Authenticate with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. Warm new IPs gradually — start with 500 sends per day and double weekly. Monitor bounce and complaint rates via the ESP dashboard; Indian ISPs such as Airtel and Jio penalize sudden spikes. Clean inactive subscribers quarterly.

## Quick checklist for founders
- Verify double opt‑in on every signup form
- Tag each contact with source and lifecycle stage
- Launch welcome, cart‑abandon, and post‑purchase flows
- Use responsive templates with clear CTAs
- Tag links with UTM parameters for revenue tracking
- Authenticate domain and monitor sender reputation
- Schedule a quarterly list hygiene session
