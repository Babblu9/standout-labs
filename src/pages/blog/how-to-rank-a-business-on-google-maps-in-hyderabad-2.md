## Claim and Verify Your Google Business Profile
Start by claiming the listing for your exact business name. Use a consistent phone number, address, and website across every platform. Verification (postcard, phone, or email) unlocks editing rights and signals legitimacy to Google.

## Optimize Core Information
- Choose the most specific primary category (e.g., "Italian restaurant" not just "restaurant").
- Add secondary categories that reflect real services.
- Write a concise description (250‑300 characters) that includes your neighbourhood and key offerings.
- Set accurate hours, including special holiday schedules.

## Collect Genuine Reviews
Ask satisfied customers directly after a positive experience. Provide a short link (via the “Get more reviews” button) to reduce friction. Respond to every review — thank the happy ones, address concerns calmly, and keep the tone professional. Consistent, recent reviews improve both ranking and conversion.

## Build Consistent Local Citations
Submit your NAP (Name, Address, Phone) to reputable Indian directories such as Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, and local chamber‑of‑commerce sites. Ensure the exact same formatting everywhere; even a missing suite number can confuse the algorithm.

## Add Photos and Posts Regularly
Upload high‑resolution images of the storefront, interior, products, and team. Aim for at least 10 photos and refresh them monthly. Use the Posts feature for offers, events, or new menu items — each post acts as fresh content that Google indexes.

## Monitor Insights and Adjust
Check the Insights tab weekly. Track views, search queries, direction requests, and call clicks. If a particular keyword drives traffic, reinforce it in your description and posts. If direction requests drop, verify that the map pin is placed correctly.

## Final Thoughts
Ranking on Google Maps is a series of small, repeatable actions rather than a one‑time trick. By keeping data accurate, encouraging real feedback, and staying active on the profile, Hyderabad businesses can steadily climb the local pack.
